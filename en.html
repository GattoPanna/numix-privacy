Numix Privacy Policy

Pre-publication draft. Revised: October 18, 2026

Publisher and contact

App: Numix. Development brand and public-facing name: GattoPanna.
GattoPanna is a brand name, not a statement of legal incorporation or a verified legal identity. Where required by law or store rules, the operator's legal name and address must be verified by the owner and provided separately before publication.
Contact: ssk37ssk37@gmail.com.

Audience

Numix is a general-audience number logic puzzle primarily intended for adults. This intended audience does not establish the age of every user. The app does not collect dates of birth.

Information stored on your device

The app stores unlocked and completed stages, circles and crosses on unfinished boards, remaining lives, endless-mode records, tutorial status, volume, language and consent-related settings. An optional display name, country code and country-change time are also stored locally. The game currently has no process that uploads ordinary saved progress to our server. Operating-system backup settings may include app data in device backups.

Advertising and consent information

The app integrates Google AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on the operating system, region, consent and identifier settings, these services may process IP addresses, approximate location, advertising or app-set identifiers, ad impressions, video views and interactions, performance and diagnostic information, and consent choices or status. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no network, identifier or diagnostic processing takes place.

Purposes include ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, consent management, and app or SDK safety and performance. Information may be sent to Google and shared with other parties. The current development build uses official test ads only; production ads remain disabled. Disclosures will be checked against the final configuration before production delivery.

Unity and other embedded features

Unity engine diagnostic data is currently enabled. Depending on version, platform and build settings, Unity may receive app or device diagnostic information relating to operation, crashes and performance. Separate Unity Analytics, Cloud Diagnostics, Unity Ads and in-app purchasing are currently disabled. Diagnostic settings and actual network behavior require verification before distribution.

Rankings and isolated testing

The normal distribution configuration keeps online rankings disabled. Explicit development/diagnostic builds now communicate with UGS Authentication, Leaderboards, Cloud Code and Cloud Save in the isolated numix-staging environment. These flows process an anonymous player ID, display name, self-selected country code, score, lives, circle/cross actions for validation, request IDs and sequence numbers. They do not upload the complete regular-stage save. The performance-test build also keeps an unsent online action transcript and its baseline board on the device, communicating primarily at board start and completion. The server replays the submitted transcript to validate lives and score.

A registered display name, flag/country code, score and rank may be visible to other users who read the leaderboard. Do not use a legal name or contact information as a display name. Country is self-declared, not verified residence inferred from location. Processing supports verified online records, leaderboard display, safe retries and abuse prevention. Unity also processes connection and diagnostic data according to its service specifications. Leaderboards collects the player ID and IP address to provide its service.

Staging data is distinct from a production launch. Settings include an online account deletion confirmation screen and support link. The automatic deletion service is currently disabled; automatic deletion is not offered in the normal build. When configured, server cleanup must be confirmed before Authentication deletion, and online submissions are blocked while deletion is pending. Failures lead to retry or support, never an unconfirmed success. This workflow keeps ordinary local stage saves. Contact the address above about deletion; do not email secret authentication credentials. Production online features will remain unpublished until deletion scope, retention periods and identity-verification procedures for Authentication, scores, private saves and logs are established. Anonymous authentication still creates server-side identifiers and is not described as collecting no account information.

Online runs accept actions for 24 hours after starting; this is not automatic server-data deletion. Profiles, best scores and the latest operation state currently have no scheduled deletion date and remain until a deletion request is processed or testing data is removed. On October 17, 2026, isolated testing verified deletion of a newly created test identity and its live leaderboard scores, minimization of private state, and replay prevention. Temporary admin permissions were removed after testing; this does not make automatic in-app deletion available in the normal build. Minimal protective deletion state and timestamps remain associated with the test ID. Their retention and removal, along with service logs and archives, require a finalized operator policy.

Consent and controls

At app launch, the app requests updated consent information from UMP and displays a form when required. When UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, an ad privacy choices button is shown in Settings. Failure to obtain consent information or load an ad does not prevent ordinary puzzle play. In diagnostic builds that offer it, ad-based revival is optional: only a legitimate rewarded-ad callback can restore three lives after game over in normal stages. The current signed distribution candidate also keeps ad revival disabled. Endless mode does not allow ad revival.

You can use operating-system controls to delete or reset advertising identifiers and manage advertising or tracking preferences. If tracking across other companies' apps or websites is introduced on iOS, required ATT permission must first be obtained. There is currently no ATT request implementation; iPhone tracking and privacy-manifest verification remain release requirements. Permission is not assumed to have been granted.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until app data is removed or otherwise reset. Administrator RESET clears progress and tutorial data, but does not delete every setting, Google's consent records or advertising data. Review your operating-system app-data and backup controls as well.

We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve a request and normally delete it within 90 days after resolution, except where legal obligations or ongoing matters require otherwise. Google and Unity retain information under their respective policies and settings; we cannot promise to erase their data on the same schedule.

For data questions or deletion requests, contact the address above. We will respond within our capabilities and direct you to the appropriate provider where information is controlled by that provider. The normal build keeps online features disabled; the diagnostic build uses anonymous authentication. Clearing local app data does not delete server-side records.

Third-party services and international processing

Service providers such as Google and Unity may process information outside your country. See:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google advertising controls: https://myadcenter.google.com/
- Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Security and updates

SDK communications use the protections offered by the respective services. Local saved data is not guaranteed to be stored encrypted. We will update the revision date when this policy changes and provide additional in-app notice for significant changes where needed.
