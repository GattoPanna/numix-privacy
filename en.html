Numix Privacy Policy

Revised: October 18, 2026

Operator and contact

App: Numix
Operator (development brand): GattoPanna

Scope

Numix is a general-audience number logic puzzle primarily intended for adults. We do not collect dates of birth. This policy includes closed testing. Online and advertising features vary by build and region.

Local storage

Regular progress, clears, circles/crosses, lives, endless mode, tutorial state, volume and language settings are stored locally. The complete regular save is not uploaded to the ranking server. OS backups may include this data. Optional nickname/country, unsent online action transcript and board, and service-notification read timestamps are also stored locally.

Online features

We use UGS Authentication, Leaderboards, Cloud Code and Cloud Save. These process anonymous player ID, optional nickname, selected country, score/lives, online actions, request IDs and sequence numbers. The transcript is sent primarily at board start/completion so the server can validate lives and score. Leaderboards collects the player ID and IP address to provide its service. Unity may also process connection and diagnostic data.

Purposes include account identification, rankings, resuming, retries and abuse prevention. Nickname, flag/country, score and rank may be visible to others. Do not use a legal name or contact information as a nickname. Country is self-declared, not verified residence. Account-related service notifications may be retrieved and displayed.

Ads, consent and diagnostics

We use Google AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and UMP. SDKs may process IP/approximate location, advertising/app-set or other identifiers, ad impressions/views/interactions, performance/diagnostics and consent information. Purposes include ad delivery/measurement, abuse prevention, consent management and safety/performance. Data may be sent to Google or shared with third parties. Non-personalized settings do not eliminate all network or identifier processing.

Closed testing uses test ads. Before production advertising we will review the configuration and policy. When available, ad revival is optional and restores three lives only after a legitimate reward callback following game over in regular stages. Endless and online modes do not offer ad revival. Consent requests and permitted preloading can occur even without pressing an ad button.

UMP updates consent on launch and displays required forms. Privacy choices can be changed in Settings when required by the SDK. Regular puzzles remain playable if consent processing or ad loading fails. OS advertising controls are also available. Current distribution targets Android.

Unity Engine diagnostics are enabled. Depending on OS/version/build, Unity may receive app/device information about operation, crashes and performance. Separate Unity Analytics, Cloud Diagnostics, Unity Ads and in-app purchasing are currently disabled.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until app data is removed or otherwise reset. Administrator RESET resets progress/tutorial, not every setting, consent or provider record. Online deletion does not erase regular-stage saves. Removing local data does not delete server records.

Online profiles, best scores and latest action state currently have no scheduled automatic deletion date and remain until deletion is processed or testing data is removed. A board's 24-hour acceptance window is not data deletion. Minimal protective state/timestamps associated with the player ID may remain after deletion to prevent unauthorized recreation or delayed request replay. These protective records currently have no scheduled automatic deletion date. A deletion request includes review of necessary protection, retention reasons and what can be removed.

Provider logs, diagnostics, audits and backups follow relevant service settings/policies. We cannot promise simultaneous or immediate erasure of every provider record. Support email is kept as needed to resolve a request and normally deleted within 90 days after resolution, except legal obligations or ongoing matters.

Deletion requests and verification

Open Settings → Delete online account to see the confirmation screen and warning. When available, confirmation starts server cleanup followed by Authentication deletion. Online submissions stop during deletion. Network errors and uncertain authentication responses are not reported as success. If the app or service is unavailable, request deletion by email through the page below.

Deletion page: https://gattopanna.github.io/numix-privacy/delete.html

Losing an anonymous session may prevent identity verification or recovery. Do not send passwords, tokens, signing keys, unnecessary addresses or identity documents. We verify the relevant record safely and refer you to the appropriate provider for provider-controlled information.
